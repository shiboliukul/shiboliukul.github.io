About me

I'm a 4th-year PhD candidate at the Thayer School of Engineering at Dartmouth College, working in Prof. Wei Ouyang's lab. My research focuses on implantable biosensors and bioelectronic devices that monitor molecules inside the body continuously and over long periods of time.
